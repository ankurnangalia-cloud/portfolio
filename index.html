01

AI-Powered Customer Support Chatbot

AI Product · Customer Experience · Process Optimisation
CSAT +20.5% Queries -20% ART -35%
Problem

Our support team was overwhelmed with repetitive queries — account issues, game rules, payment questions. CSAT was sitting at 3.4 out of 5, and Average Resolution Time was climbing. Users were waiting too long for help, leading to churn and frustration at critical moments in the game journey.

Action

I owned the end-to-end build of an AI-powered support chatbot. I started by analysing 3 months of support tickets to identify the top 12 query categories. I designed conversation flows for each, defined query classification logic, and built human escalation triggers for edge cases. I worked with engineering on flows integration and with operations on the agent handoff workflow. I defined success metrics before a single line of code was written.

Result
  • Reduced support query volume by 20% through automated resolution
  • Improved CSAT from 3.4 to 4.1 — a 20.5% improvement — across 100K+ monthly users
  • Reduced Average Resolution Time (ART) by 35%
  • Recognised with the 'Dynamic Duo' award for delivery excellence

🔍 Key Learning

The most impactful decision was starting with ticket data analysis, not user interviews. The patterns in 90 days of tickets told us exactly what to automate, skipping that step would have led us to build flows for assumed problems instead of real ones.

02

Revenue Feature — Consumer Engagement

Consumer Product · Revenue Growth · Engagement
7.6% Daily Revenue 100K+ Users ARPU +5%
Problem

User engagement on the platform was plateauing for our Arcade Vertical. We had strong migration numbers from the core games but returning user sessions were declining and ARPU was flat. The business needed a consumer feature that would both drive engagement and contribute directly to daily revenue — not just a nice-to-have improvement.

Action

I ran discovery sessions with 30+ users across different player cohorts to understand what drove them to return and transact. I analysed cohort data to identify patterns in high-value user behaviour. I translated insights which led us to introduce a leaderboard completly based on tangible rewards, wrote the PRD, managed agile delivery across engineering and design, and launched the first cashback leaderboard.

Result
  • Feature contributed 7.6% to daily platform revenue within 4 weeks of launch
  • Reached 100,000+ monthly active users within 3 months
  • Drove 5% ARPU growth through data-driven engagement design
  • Feature became a permanent part of the core product roadmap

🔍 Key Learning

Users don't return for features — they return for rewards they can see and feel. The moment we anchored the leaderboard to tangible cashback rewards, the value proposition became concrete. Which taught me that in real-money gaming, motivation needs to be visible and immediate — perceived reward drives behaviour faster than any UX improvement alone.

03

B2B Partner Integration & Third-Party Network

B2B Product · API Integration · Revenue
Revenue +6% Zero post-launch issues
Problem

The platform needed a new revenue channel that didn't require building new consumer products from scratch. A partnership with a third party who can with whom we can work on revenue sharing basis — but it required a complex API integration, tight coordination between internal and external teams, and a product spec precise enough that both sides could build against it.

Action

I took complete ownership of the B2B onboarding. I ran discovery sessions with the partner to document their technical requirements, translated those into internal requirements for our engineering team, and wrote the API integration spec. I managed the testing phase — defining test cases, coordinating UAT between both teams, and tracking blockers through to resolution. I owned the go-live checklist and signed off on launch readiness.

Result
  • Delivered integration on time with zero post-launch critical issues
  • Drove 6% incremental revenue through the structured partner network
  • Created a repeatable onboarding playbook used for future partner integrations

🔍 Key Learning

The biggest risk in B2B integrations is ambiguity in requirements — what each side thinks has been agreed often differs. Writing a shared API spec that both teams signed off on before development started eliminated most of the late-stage surprises.
